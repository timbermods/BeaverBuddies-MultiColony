# Credits

**Timber Together is a modified version of BeaverBuddies**, the Timberborn co-op mod created by
**Thomas Price ([thomaswp](https://github.com/thomaswp))**. The multiplayer at its heart is theirs: keeping every
player's game in step tick by tick, sending each player's actions to the host and replaying them everywhere, the
Steam and direct-IP connections, the desync checks, and much of the code in this mod. Timber Together would not exist
without that work.

- With contributions from Robin, Slide (Cilyena), Phil Lehmkuhl, SamuZad, Joe Stead, Zibo Ye, Dasker and Tarensaror,

Every commit of the original keeps its author in this repository's history.

## BeaverBuddies Stability Fork

A fork of BeaverBuddies by Timbermods that fixes crashes and desyncs (water, animation, random numbers, saving) and
adds Steam friend invites over Valve's current networking, the connection panel with chat, and player cursors.
Timber Together is built on it.

- Source: https://github.com/timbermods/BeaverBuddies-Stability-Fork

## Timber Together

Separate colonies on one map, Trading Posts, the Co-op Game room, Folktails and Iron Teeth together, and the colony
checks, by [Timbermods](https://github.com/timbermods).

- Source: https://github.com/timbermods/TimberTogether
- Changes: [STABILITY-CHANGELOG.md](STABILITY-CHANGELOG.md), and the repository's history
