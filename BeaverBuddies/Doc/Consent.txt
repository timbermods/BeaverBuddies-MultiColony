Consent to share usage data

If you agree, the following from this game session is sent to the developers of this mod:

- Log data: collected automatically while you play, such as game events and what you do in the game.
- Save file: your current game save.

We try to keep this data mostly anonymous. But because it is sent as it is, it may include details that could identify you (such as file paths or usernames). The developers won't try to identify you from it.

Your choices:
- If you agree, the data is sent securely to the developers and used to improve the mod.
- If you don't agree, nothing is sent, and your game carries on as before.

By continuing, you confirm that you are at least the legal age of digital consent where you live.
