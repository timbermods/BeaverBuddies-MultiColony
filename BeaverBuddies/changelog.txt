v1.4.0-beta5 (BeaverBuddies MultiColony)
* A speed boost at the top of the chat box: - and + (or a typed number) add a constant to the speed picked at the top right, for everyone in the game, so the fastest button (7x) with +0.5 runs at 7.5x. From -6.5 to +23; the game never runs below 0.5x or above 30x. A guest catching up above speed 7 is no longer capped below its speed.
* Full details for every version: STABILITY-CHANGELOG.md in the mod folder.

v1.4.0-beta4 (BeaverBuddies MultiColony)
* From the Stability Fork 1.1.11: every player gets a colour of their own on the cursors and in the chat until they choose one (the host orange, then blue, green, pink, purple, teal, red, lime), and only the name is coloured in the chat. Colony colours stay the game's own.
* Full details for every version: STABILITY-CHANGELOG.md in the mod folder.

v1.4.0-beta3
* The Mod Settings tooltips are one or two short lines each and no longer run off the screen.

v1.4.0-beta2
* The trading window (Ctrl+T) shows each colony's food and water with the days they last, what it is looking for (set yours there, up to three things), and how many days of the host's limit an absent player has missed; everyone is warned the day before a hand-over.
* Ask a friend to look after your colony: they switch into it and back from the trading window, and it is not handed over while they are in the game.
* Click a player in the connection panel to take your camera to them; the Home key brings you back to your colony.
* A Trading Post remembers its last exchange (Offer again; a ledger row puts a round's terms in the form); Decline leaves the offer's terms in your form for a counter-offer; Keep at least sets a reserve so a standing deal never starves you.
* The host is asked before its first change while players can still join; a guest's change then is refused; the panel says Joining: open.

v1.4.0-beta1 (BeaverBuddies MultiColony)
* A performance pass with the guest (player 2) in mind: events are hashed and compressed once and never written out twice; Steam is served before the game's frame; cursor frames go out only when something changed; the profiler, working-hours, placement-preview and top-bar patches no longer take a lock or allocate on every call. Nothing simulated changed.
* The mod list and the two settings now say (beta).
* Full details for every version: STABILITY-CHANGELOG.md in the mod folder.

v1.4.0-alpha14

v1.4.0-alpha13
* A running digest of every colony state change is sent with each heartbeat; a guest whose digest differs stops the tick it happens, with both numbers in the log.
* The daily colony check now covers unlock names, contested land, every exchange, the goods on each half, the totals, the mode flags and the slot table, in every co-op game.

v1.4.0-alpha12
* Two colonies' roads joined no longer freeze the game; placing beside another colony's unfinished path is refused; a Trading Post's halves are accepted or refused together, and only its partners may remove it.
* Hand-over by absence works with detailed logging on; a guest who leaves is away from the next day; Ctrl+Shift+K needs nobody connected.
* A guest's dev-mode unlock waits for the host; log piles go to the colony that may work there; traded beavers must be able to walk there and carry nothing; copying settings from another colony's building is refused; a refused working-hours change resets the panel.

v1.4.0-alpha11
* The Trading Post reworked: each side's goods wait on its own half until both are in, then everything crosses at once; science is traded like a good; up to 100 an item a round, with rounds; ending an exchange takes both colonies; a ledger per post; a native trading window.
* The desync review's fixes: founding and hand-over wait for the host's first tick, and the first change while paused closes joining; dev mode's Ctrl keys are off in co-op; relic science goes to the right colony; founding uses the host's starting settings; a guest missing the Trading Post's files is refused; the daily colony check is compared between computers.
* A guest's placements come back faster: the host checks a placement once and guests take its answer.

v1.4.0-alpha10

v1.4.0-alpha1 to alpha9
